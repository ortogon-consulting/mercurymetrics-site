02 - Observation

Same title.
Different value.

Two RMs can serve similar clients, hold the same role and both perform strongly while creating value in fundamentally different ways.

Momentum
One RM creates movement by opening new relationships. Opportunity begins with access, energy and forward motion.
Relevance
Another becomes indispensable by understanding complexity. Value emerges through judgment, substance and difficult questions.
Trust
Another is invited in before the financial question is even defined. The relationship creates access to the real decision underneath.

04 - Relevance

Why it matters.

The interesting question is not which archetype is "best", but where the way you naturally create value is most useful and where another orientation may matter more.

01
Different situations reward different strengths.

The way you naturally operate can be especially valuable in some client moments and less useful in others.

02
Strengths can shape blind spots.

The approaches we rely on most naturally are often the ones we reach for first even when another approach might serve the situation better.

03
Fit matters.

Clients, roles and environments can reinforce the way you create value or leave part of it underused.
